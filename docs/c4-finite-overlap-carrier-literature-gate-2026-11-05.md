## Proposed claim and experiment

For complete paired proper-prefix transitions
`(c,c',z) -> (d,d',M^-q(z+p'-p))`, prove an invariant physical strip and
combine it with a certified stable enclosure to obtain an explicit finite
integer carrier. The proposed sharper invariant, from zero-offset roots, is
`-ell_c < ell z < ell_c'`. A nonempty overlap also satisfies
`Wz in Q-Q`; invertibility of `Phi=(ell,W)` therefore bounds every integer
coordinate. A proved geometric exclusion is distinct from a resource cap.

Apply the resulting arithmetic to a genuinely different incidence matrix:

```text
M = [[0,1,1],[2,1,1],[0,1,0]], det M=2,
chi(x)=x^3-x^2-3x-2,
sigma(0)=11, sigma(1) in Perm(012), sigma(2) in {01,10}.
```

This is a complete twelve-source class containing the repository's
`11/210/10` specimen. Authenticate its Perron chart, full occurrence digits,
contracting block and enclosure separately. Attempt exact complete graph
closure and positive subinvariant certificates; record inconclusive cases
honestly if a resource or weight-search budget is exhausted.

Also check whether within-letter occurrence-piece disjointness upgrades the
measure relation of a closed paired graph to exact equality
`v=beta^-q T v`. If established, a positive overlap requires a reachable
Perron-critical strongly connected component; the converse is not assumed.

## Primary sources inspected and hypothesis boundaries

1. **Akiyama--Lee, Algorithm for determining pure pointedness of self-affine
   tilings**, [arXiv:1003.2898](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1003.2898), the
   potential-overlap bounds (2.8)--(2.10), the multigraph discussion and
   Theorem 4.1. Finite potential-overlap graphs and spectral comparisons are
   established methods. Their spectral/PDS equivalence assumes a self-affine
   tiling and the Meyer property. We do not import that equivalence: our
   finite-carrier proof uses the explicit integral lattice and invertible
   chart of #494.
2. **Minervino--Thuswaldner, The geometry of non-unit Pisot substitutions**,
   [arXiv:1402.2002](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.2002), Section 2.1,
   Lemma 3.1 and Theorem 8.3. Periodic substitution points, non-unit internal
   measure scaling and occurrence-indexed set equations supply the closest
   representation-space context. The repository proves its own compact
   matrix-adic model; no identification with the full localized adelic space,
   degree-one tiling or PDS conclusion is assumed.
3. **Thuswaldner, Unimodular Pisot substitutions and their associated
   tiles** (2006), [primary PDF](https://www.numdam.org/item/JTNB_2006__18_2_487_0.pdf),
   Lemma 3.4, pp. 507--508, and Definition 3.6. Bounding both the Perron
   coordinate and the contracting coordinates to prove effective contact
   graph stabilization is direct prior art. Its unit/contact setup and
   later super-coincidence hypotheses are not transferred to our non-unit
   compact lift; the integer carrier is proved directly here.
4. **Hutchinson, Fractals and self similarity**,
   [author-hosted paper](https://maths-people.anu.edu.au/~john/Assets/Research%20Papers/fractals_self-similarity.pdf),
   Sections 3.1(3) and 3.2. The compact-set contraction/fixed-point mechanism
   is standard. For our graph-directed operator, a common contracting power
   is proved in the product Hausdorff metric, with the compact matrix-adic
   factor included. This permits a common one-step attractor across different
   prefix periods; it does not provide a uniform spectral contraction for
   every overlap graph.

The strip, bounded-lattice, Perron and contraction ingredients are standard.
The intended contribution is their explicit repository-level assembly,
exact finite carrier certificate, and a separately authenticated new
incidence experiment. No general novelty claim is made.

## Controls and reasons to stop

- Preserve every paired occurrence, including repeated targets. Deleted
  multiplicities change the measure equation and invalidate the certificate.
- A nonintegral candidate is excluded only by exact membership in M^q Z^3.
- Interval uncertainty and boundary contact are retained. A numerical point
  outside Q-Q is not a certified geometric exclusion.
- The physical strip is a reachability invariant for admitted roots. It is
  not an emptiness theorem for every arbitrary shifted internal intersection.
- An unproved coordinate/state cap yields an inconclusive run. Mathematical
  finiteness does not make an exhausted operational budget an acceptance.
- Diagonal self-overlaps have positive measure; a globally strict
  contraction assertion for every possible root would be false.
- A critical graph component is only a necessary condition for positive
  overlap. Eigenvalues alone do not establish its geometric realization.
- Existing #496 chart coefficients, quotient arithmetic and rate thresholds
  belong to its old matrix and must not be reused for the new matrix without
  re-authentication.
