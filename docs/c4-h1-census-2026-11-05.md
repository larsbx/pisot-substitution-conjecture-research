# A `dim H¹` census of the PIP corpus through the Barge–Diamond complex — 2026-11-05

**Status:** stop/go literature gate (AGENTS.md) and finite-domain census. The
formula is a theorem of Barge–Diamond 2008 (Theorem 4; Barge 2015 exact
sequence (4.1)) specialised over `ℚ`; the census is an exact computation
confirmed by construction-independent Anderson–Putnam computations on every
specimen. It certifies no pure discrete spectrum, bounds no coincidence rank
from above, decides nothing for any individual specimen's spectrum, and
changes no claim status. Companion of the
[cut-attractor note](c4-cut-attractor-window-2026-11-05.md), whose open
quantifier lives at coincidence rank 2.
[Issue #461](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/issues/461).

## 1. Proposed computation and why

The cut-attractor note left one concrete finite step: Barge 2015 Theorem 22
says that a Pisot substitution with dilation of odd norm and coincidence rank
2 has `dim H¹(Ω_σ; ℚ) ≥ 2d − 1 = 5`. So a census of `dim H¹` over the
standing corpus says, specimen by specimen, whether the `cr = 2` wall
(Proposition R there: at `cr = 2`, a strict system exists iff two subtiles
have intersecting interiors) can occur at all.

## 3. The formula

Let `σ` be primitive and aperiodic on `n` letters. The vertex-edge graph `S`
has vertices `x⁺` and `y⁻` and one edge `e_{xy}: x⁺ → y⁻` per legal
two-letter word `xy`; `f(e_{xy}) = e_{last(σx) first(σy)}`. Let `ER` be the
eventual range of `f` on the edges (the stable set of the decreasing chain
`fⁿ(legal)`), with `E` edges, `V` vertices and `k` components. Tensoring
Barge's (4.1) with `ℚ`:

```text
dim G1 = evrank(Mᵀ),   dim G0 = k − 1,   dim G3 = b₁(ER) = E − V + k,
dim H¹(Ω_σ; ℚ) = dim G3 + dim G1 − dim G0 = evrank(Mᵀ) + E − V + 1.
```

`G3 = H¹(ER)` is the Barge–Diamond proof step: `f` permutes the edges of
`ER`, so a power of `f` is homotopic to the identity on `ER`, and the eventual
range of `f*` on `H¹(S)` is `H¹(ER)`. The formula holds for every `k`
(Barge–Diamond Example 8 has `k = 2`, `evrank = 4`, `dim 3`).

*Legal two-letter words.* The two-factors of the images, closed under
`xy → last(σx) first(σy)`, are exactly the legal two-letter words: a
two-factor of `σⁿ(i) = σ(b₁)⋯σ(b_m)` lies inside some `σ(b_j)` or straddles
`σ(b_j)σ(b_{j+1})` with `b_j b_{j+1}` a two-factor of `σ^{n−1}(i)`; conversely
image two-factors are legal by primitivity and the legal two-factors are
closed under the map. Checked against brute force on all 4,554 specimens;
the first `n` at which the two-factors of `σⁿ(a)` reach the closure has
histogram `{1: 48, 2: 2586, 3: 1608, 4: 180, 5: 96, 6: 24, 7: 12}`, so a
brute-force regression needs `n ≥ 7`.

*Corpus specialisation.* An irreducible cubic has no eigenvalue `0` or `1`, so
`evrank = 3` and `k = 1` on every specimen (both verified exactly on all
4,554): `dim H¹ = 3 + l`, `l = E − V + 1` the asymptotic-cycle rank.

## 5. The census

`kernel/psc/h1_census.mojo` (canonical) over `pip_corpus()`;
`oracles/python/h1_census_ap.py` (Anderson–Putnam 1-collared complex, two
vertex presentations, no shared construction) agrees on every specimen; in
the audit workflow, seven further independent computations (full
Barge–Diamond cochain complex, Barge–Diamond Theorem 4 count, 2-collared
Anderson–Putnam) agreed on all 4,554 with zero disagreements.

`ER` is connected on every specimen; its `(E, V)` shapes are
`(1,2): 780, (2,3): 1344, (3,4): 918, (4,4): 504, (4,5): 60, (5,5): 48,
(6,5): 708, (7,6): 12, (9,6): 180`.

The rank is governed by the endpoint-map types `A..G` of
`docs/c4-endpoint-core-program.md` (`σ₊`, `σ₋`):

- if either map is eventually constant (types A, B), `ER` is a star into one
  vertex, `l = 0` and `dim H¹ = 3`; this covers all 3,012 specimens of the
  endpoint-collapse stratum of the cut-attractor note;
- `dim 4` occurs only for `(C,E), (C,F), (E,C), (F,C), (F,F)`;
- `dim 5` only for `(C,G), (D,F), (E,E), (E,F), (F,D), (F,E), (F,G), (G,C), (G,F)`;
- `dim 7` only for `(D,G), (E,G), (G,D), (G,E)`, e.g. `1/02/220`, where both
  maps are permutations, all nine two-letter words are legal and `ER = K₃,₃`.

The full `(σ₊ type, σ₋ type, dim)` table is pinned in both the Mojo regression
and the oracle.

## 6. What the census means

- (conditional on Barge 2015 Thm 22 as read) On the corpus `det ∈ {−1, 1, 2}`,
  so "norm odd" is exactly "unimodular". Among the 2,628 unimodular specimens,
  **2,136 have `dim H¹ ≤ 4`, where Theorem 22 is incompatible with coincidence
  rank 2**, and 492 have `dim H¹ ≥ 5` (408 at 5, 84 at 7), where Theorem 22
  imposes no restriction. The `cr = 2` wall of the cut-attractor note can
  therefore live only among those 492.
- Among the 1,740 unimodular specimens with `dim H¹ = 3` (the homological
  Pisot ones, `l = 0`), Theorem 22, equivalently Corollary 24, is incompatible
  with coincidence rank 2, so a failure of pure discrete spectrum there would
  have coincidence rank at least 3. **Do not cite Corollary 25 for this**:
  `l = 0` is not "no asymptotic cycle" in Barge's sense (Fibonacci has `l = 0`
  and a doubly asymptotic pair).
- Theorem 22 says nothing about the 1,926 specimens with `det = 2` (even
  norm).

Forbidden wording: "excludes `cr = 2` for specimen X" as a property of the
specimen (say "Theorem 22 is incompatible with `cr = 2` there"); "rank >
degree iff asymptotic cycle"; "homological Pisot implies PDS"; any PDS,
PSC, HPC or C4 claim for any specimen.

## 7. Decision

**Proceed, as a stratification.** No novelty is claimed for the formula,
which is Barge–Diamond's theorem read over `ℚ`. The repository value is the
exact per-specimen table, the identification of the strata by endpoint-map
type, and the placement of the `cr = 2` wall inside 492 unimodular specimens.
