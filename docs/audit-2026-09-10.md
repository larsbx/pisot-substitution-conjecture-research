### B.1 Free-Lie membership of the first defect (item 9)

The note says this is "not yet formalized". The argument is right and worth
writing down once, because D2 of `verification-architecture.md` shows the
naive shuffle identity is false: scattered-subword counts are a character of
the *infiltration* product, `w₁ ↑ w₂ = w₁ ⧢ w₂ + (terms of degree < |w₁|+|w₂|)`.
At the first nonzero degree `r` all lower defects vanish, so the lower-degree
correction terms cancel and `K_r` is orthogonal to every shuffle product of
lower degrees; by Ree's theorem that is exactly the free-Lie sector. The Lean
file proves only the `r = 3`, `K₂ = 0` instance.

### B.2 Galois-orbit bookkeeping (items 11–13)

Two implicit points, both fine:

- In the `A₃` branch of `W₃`, the two cubic factors `P₊, P₋` are distinct
  (their root sets differ), so the primary components are simple. Even if they
  coincided, the spectral-radius conclusion would survive, because any nonzero
  invariant subspace of an isotypic component with all eigenvalues of modulus
  `> β` has radius `> β`.
- The mod-3 sieve computes the maximum modulus over the *Galois orbit of an
  eigenvalue*, which is the right quantity: a rational invariant subspace has
  Galois-stable spectrum. Coincidences of eigenvalues across different
  multidegrees of the same total degree are therefore irrelevant to the
  theorem (and do not occur for Pisot cubics: a relation
  `β^p α^q γ^s = 1`, `p+q+s = 0`, forces `p = q = s = 0` in both Galois
  branches).

### B.3 The three-state normal forms are probably vacuous

Items 10 (three-state clause), 12 §8, 13 §7–8, 15, 17, 18 all assume
`|C| = 3`. But item 14 forces `|C| ≥ 4` in five of eight parity classes and
`|C| ≥ 6` in two of them, and in the corpus the smallest recurrent SCC among
the 546 arithmetic survivors has size 4. Nothing in the program suggests a
minimal counterexample would have three states. These results are useful as
calibration for the sieve layers; they are not attack surface. §D.2 returns
to this.

## D. Strategic assessment

### D.1 G1 is untouched, and it may not be a side hypothesis

Nothing has been done on G1 since the v5 Theorem 5.1 retraction. The archive
records the bridge direction only (BPA termination with coincidence ⟹ PDS,
via Barge–Štimac–Williams). It does not record whether the converse is a
theorem. My recollection is that Sirvent–Solomyak (2002) state an equivalence
for irreducible Pisot substitutions, in which case

    PDS  ⟹  B_σ finite,

so G1 is a *necessary consequence* of the conjecture, `G1 ∧ C1` is a
reformulation of PSC rather than a reduction of it, and the whole C4 program,
even if completed, proves PSC only on the set where G1 is separately known
(`|A| = 2` and the verified families). This should be settled by citation and
recorded in the ledger before more effort goes into C4. If the converse holds,
G1 deserves at least equal weight.

### D.2 Sieve saturation

The last nine PRs follow one pattern: take a golden negative that passes every
existing necessary condition, add a finer invariant that rejects it, pin it in
CI, repeat. Each layer is correct, but no layer comes with a completeness
statement, and the golden negative is a single object. This process has no
natural termination: for fixed `σ` and G1, existence of `C` is already
decidable by building `B_σ`, so a sieve layer adds proof value only if it is
*uniform in `σ`*. The uniform content so far is thin exactly in the live case
`r = 2`: `Λ²M` is a quotient of `S`, and a parity bound on `|C|`. Everything
else in the degree-2 program is `|C| = 3`-specific (B.3).

### D.3 Two proved tools the program has not used

- **Pisot contraction has entered only through eigenvalue moduli**
  (`ρ(Λ²M) < β`, the mod-3 sieve). It has never been applied to the object
  where the synthetic obstruction lives: the prefix-difference walk
  `D_T(k) = Parikh(u[:k]) − Parikh(v[:k])`, whose return times *are* the child
  boundaries. In a finite closed `C` all states have bounded length while
  `σⁿ(T)` has length `≍ βⁿ`, so the inflated walk returns to `0` at bounded
  gaps; a uniform argument should couple that return density to the
  contraction of `D` in the stable directions. C4's original statement asked
  for exactly this and it remains the unused ingredient.
- **Unique decodability / recognizability** is theorem-grade at Level 2 and
  is never invoked in C4. In the gauge-trivial case one has two distinct
  Parikh-equal word morphisms `U, V` with `σ∘U = U∘τ_C` and `σ∘V = V∘τ_C`;
  the notes correctly say that child cuts are not yet known to align with
  `σ`-supertile boundaries. That alignment lemma is the natural next
  *theorem* (not sieve) target, and Mossé recognizability is the natural tool.

### D.4 Verification layers

The Lean layer covers the seed-level finite algebra only; none of the C4
results is formalized, and two of the informal steps (B.1, semisimplicity of
`Φ₃`) are load-bearing for items 9–13. The TLA+ layer is stale (A.1.1). The
Mojo/CI layer is the only one that actually verifies the new material, and it
verifies censuses, not proofs.
