## 2. Uniform local two-generation spanning statement

Let `sigma` be a nonerasing substitution on the three-letter alphabet
with integer incidence `M` whose characteristic polynomial
`chi_M` is **irreducible over Q**. Let `T=(u,v)` be any nonempty
Parikh-balanced finite word pair, not necessarily irreducible or legal.
For `j=0,1,2`, define `D_j(T)` to be the actual **multiset of ordered balanced blocks at depth j**:

- `D_0(T)` is the one unreduced whole balanced pair `T`;
- `D_1(T)` consists of the genuine ordered zero-return factors of
  `sigma(T)`;
- `D_2(T)` consists of the ordered zero-return factors of the
  inflation of **every occurrence** in `D_1(T)`, preserving all
  parent ordinals, child ordinals, diagonal children and repeated
  occurrences.

Write `p(S)=Parikh(top(S))=Parikh(bottom(S))` and `p=p(T)`.

> **Two-generation spanning:** The union
> `D_0(T) ⊔ D_1(T) ⊔ D_2(T)` contains three *actual*
> occurrence-labelled blocks whose Parikh columns have
> a nonzero rational (indeed integer) `3×3` determinant.

### Proof

Actual ordered balanced-factor decomposition partitions the whole
inflated top word. Therefore, counting with occurrence multiplicity,

```text
sum_{S in D_j(T)} p(S) = M^j p,     for j=0,1,2.
```

This is an equality of integer vectors, not a statement about
abstract normalized BPA adjacency. The `j=2` identity follows by
applying the same equality to *each occurrence* of a first-generation
child and concatenating the resulting actual blocks.

Now `p` is a nonzero rational vector because `u` is nonempty.
If `p,Mp,M²p` were linearly dependent, a nonzero polynomial
`f` of degree at most two would satisfy `f(M)p=0`.
The monic polynomial of least degree annihilating `p` divides
`chi_M`. Since `chi_M` is irreducible of degree three and
`p != 0`, that least polynomial is `chi_M`, contradiction.

Consequently `p,Mp,M²p` form a rational basis of `Q³`.
Each of these vectors is an integer sum of the actual descendant
columns in the corresponding level, so their combined column span
must also equal `Q³`. Selecting three independent members of this
finite multiset gives a nonzero integer minor. QED.

**General dimension:** for an alphabet of size `d` with irreducible
degree-`d` incidence characteristic polynomial, the same argument
gives `d` independent actual descendant Parikh vectors within
levels `0,...,d-1`. The current executable is specialized
to three letters and does not establish a finite-state theorem
for other alphabets.

### Finite-index refinement (no new premise)

Let `L_T` be the integer lattice generated by *all* actual
depth-at-most-two descendant Parikh columns. The displayed sum
identities show that `p,Mp,M²p` belong to `L_T`; these three
vectors form an invertible integer matrix `K=[p,Mp,M²p]`.
Consequently

```text
K Z³ ⊆ L_T ⊆ Z³,
[Z³ : L_T] divides |det K|.
```

This is the elementary subgroup index formula, without a claim
that `L_T` itself is the whole integer lattice in general.
If a *literal* independent triple has determinant `±1`, it
already certifies `L_T=Z³`. In the real-secondary PIP
`1/02/202` control below, the observed exact minor is `+1`,
so **its depth-at-most-two descendant Parikh columns generate the entire
integer lattice**, stronger than mere rational rank.
Neither this finite-index bound nor saturation makes an
interior newborn boundary synchronizing. In particular,
integer span of *block masses* is not an occurrence-address
or non-unit realized-overlap dictionary.

## 3. Consequence specifically for a hypothetical strict C4 SCC

If a finite strict child-closed ternary PIP component `C` is
**supplied**, then the three independent actual descendant
occurrences from **every** `T in C` must lie entirely in `C`
(after raw-orientation normalization). Thus:

> **Two-step rank necessity:** From every strict source state,
> its full depth-at-most-two actual child-occurrence cone
> contains three rationally independent Parikh types.

This strictly strengthens the previously established *global*
`rank(P_C)=3` bookkeeping: it bounds **where** the independent
columns must occur in the actual child graph, rather than just
saying that three states somewhere in the SCC span.

**Still missing:** A rank-three local cone does not force any
child boundary into `Sync_+` or `Sync_-`, prove that a
nonproductive failure yields a finite strict component (G1), or
supply any hub-residual word/recognizability alignment.
There is no claim that all descendant lineages converge or that
finite local ranks prove C4.

### Strict PIP consequence and next obligation

In a supplied strict child-closed PIP component, every root's own cone
through two steps contains three distinct normalized state types with
independent masses. More generally, every consecutive three-level
window `j,j+1,j+2` has rank three and lattice index dividing
`|det(M)|^j |det K|`, since its aggregate columns are `M^j K`.
Thus no nonzero rational linear functional can vanish on all the
masses in any such window. This is a constraint on complete cones,
including all occurrences, and leaves endpoint synchronization open.

[#459](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/pull/459)
rules out deducing a synchronizing newborn from strict closure, literal
integral-word ancestry, positive occurrence signs and all-child hub
flatness under primitive nonsingular incidence. Its 16 residuals
vanish for both compatible hubs while all 12 newborns evade both
endpoint synchronizations. First-child flatness and finite good-edge
mask compatibility cannot repair that implication; the mask only
checks necessary compatibility. The fixture lies in a common rational
plane and has reducible incidence. The rank-three flat-word case,
genuine good-edge coincidence, and extraction of a finite strict
component from a failing seed remain separate obligations.

Next, test the following sufficient bridge on the *literal* strict
birth atlas: under genuine PIP and the relevant good-edge hypotheses,
does flatness together with the absence of synchronizing newborns force
a single nonzero rational functional to annihilate every Parikh column
in one complete three-level cone? Compute the kernel on each complete
cone and demand a derivation of a common functional from the ordered
cut and endpoint constraints, including transport through both child
steps. The `p0−p2` functional is the reducible calibration. A separate
functional per cut, a functional of prefix differences only, or a
functional that vanishes on just a selected lineage is insufficient.
If established, the bridge would exclude the flat PIP case. Applying
it to arbitrary strict components would still require a justified
reduction to flatness or a separate argument for nonflat words.
