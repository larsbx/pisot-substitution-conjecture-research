# Source review for the C4 periodic-ray decision

This review precedes the implementation for [issue #461](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/issues/461). The proposed result is an exact decision for existence of a finite strict fully child-closed system for a **fixed** primitive irreducible Pisot substitution. It combines the repository's periodic first-factor reduction with coincidence and common-boundary reachability on the actual prefix-aligned overlap graph. The decision ranges over every component size and permits non-unit incidence.

| Source | Applicable result and hypothesis boundary |
| --- | --- |
| [Bernat, 2008, §3.1](https://doi.org/10.1051/ita:2007062) | Complete reduction of balanced words and literal iteration of every factor are established. Finiteness of a chosen balanced-pair algorithm remains a separate condition. |
| [Sirvent–Solomyak, 2002, Lemma 5.5 and Theorem 5.6](https://doi.org/10.4153/CMB-2002-062-3) | Rationally independent tile lengths identify simultaneous tile boundaries with balanced cuts. Uniform half-coincidence gaps correspond to bounded factor lengths. Their theorem starts with a fixed-point prefix and a translate. Here there are two different rays of the same substitution, fixed by a common power and aligned at their origins. The gap proof must be given for that setting. |
| [Barge–Diamond, 2002, Theorem 1](https://doi.org/10.24033/bsmf.2433) | For an irreducible Pisot substitution some distinct aligned source letters are eventually coincident. That genuinely witnessed edge can prune ternary roots. The decision should test all cyclic first-letter pairs and therefore does not need the edge as input. |
| [Akiyama–Lee, 2011](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.2898) | Finite overlap graphs and coincidence reachability are established in their setting. The construction here uses only the integral relative prefix digits of a particular pair of literal rays. It imports no identification with the hull overlap graph and no spectral implication. |

The repository already states the strict-system equivalence with bounded-gap, tile-disjoint periodic rays in [the cut-attractor note, §5](c4-cut-attractor-window-2026-11-05.md#5-strict-systems-are-a1-plus-bounded-gaps). The [overlap-depth note](bpa-termination-by-overlap-depth-2026-10-02.md#5-proposition-4-the-half-coincidence-strengthening) bounds factor sizes under half-coincidence reachability for swap seeds. The [periodic-pair box criterion](p1b-vertex-coincidence-box-2026-10-02.md#2-proposition-v-the-box-graph) asks whether bi-infinite periodic tilings at common interior centers share some vertex. The proposed application binds the original prefix source pair and decides bounded gaps throughout its two rays. Endpoint witnesses include the **closed** overlap's right endpoint: an interior-only or left-alignment-only readout can give the wrong local boundary predicate.

**Decision.** Proceed with this combination and give both implications. A full source decision requires a complete reachable graph. An exhausted budget, synthetic edge, touch-only edge, or absent stem is insufficient. In a non-Pisot control, equal physical lengths can fail to imply equal Parikh prefixes, or offsets can leave a finite box. Retain these limitations and the earlier productive finite-cone and strict reducible controls. Credit neither a new general coincidence theorem nor extraction from arbitrary seed failure.
