The challenge is a uniform, sound coincidence/productivity proof across contraction directions, legal occurrence paths and, for non-unit substitutions, finite-place representation factors.

## 1. The valid core versus the abandoned v9/v10 implications

For any substitution on `d` letters, `det(M_sigma) != 0` implies the `d` image words form a code: if they did not, the classical defect theorem would embed them in a submonoid generated by at most `d-1` words; then their Parikh vectors (the columns of `M_sigma`) would span a proper rational subspace. The same argument applies to every power since `det(M_sigma^n)=det(M_sigma)^n != 0`. No Pisot property, primitivity, unimodularity or closed-form conjugates are needed **for this code theorem**. Unique parsing gives a lower-level hierarchy *inside an explicitly specified supertile*, not identification of entire pointed bi-infinite tilings.

The historical step `UD -> bounded total padding -> finite irreducible BPA -> no recurrent noncoincident cycle -> PDS` is **not established**. The [v9 extraction audit](v9-extraction-audit-2026-09-23.md) already withdraws predecessor contraction, global witness injectivity and diameter-only preservation. Backward length contraction does not bound arbitrary forward-reachable states; equality inside a finite supertile does not identify the external tiling; and a supertile's diameter exceeding a displacement does not prevent two points from straddling its boundary.

**Exact minimal countercalibration — Fibonacci.** For `sigma(a)=ab, sigma(b)=a`, with `M=[[1,1],[1,0]]`, determinant `-1` and irreducible Pisot polynomial `x^2-x-1`, inflation and irreducible balanced-pair decomposition yield:

```text
(ab, ba) -> (aba, aab) = (a,a) | (ba,ab)
(ba, ab) -> (aab, aba) = (a,a) | (ab,ba)
```

Therefore the noncoincident states `(ab,ba)` and `(ba,ab)` form a directed two-cycle **which emits coincidence siblings**. This directly defeats the historical universal claim "there are no recurrent noncoincident cycles"; it does **not** refute Fibonacci PDS or relevant productive-overlap criteria. The repository already has an analogous flipped-Tribonacci productive recurrent-cycle control in its v9 audit. For such graphs, the relevant property is **eventual coincidence/productivity from the specified reachable states**, not acyclicity of the noncoincident subgraph. Bare cycles can be productive.

**Three non-equivalent objects:** (i) code-theoretic UD of complete image concatenations; (ii) finite irreducible BPA/G1 for specified swap seeds; (iii) the separately proved *finite seed-patch overlap graph*, for which productivity remains open. In particular, the present shortest PDS route does **not** assume finite BPA.

## 3. High-degree technical consequences for the exact kernel

1. **Exact numbers versus dynamics:** `Q(beta)` operations, Sturm chains, rational isolating intervals and Perron-field identities settle equality/sign queries; they alone do not turn a projected affine state into a legal, occurrence-indexed overlap or show it has a coincidence descendant.
2. **Pisot enters after UD:** contraction/bounded discrepancy yields an exact *finite seed-overlap* state universe in the live proof. It does not force all reachable states to be productive, nor finite irreducible BPA automatically.
3. **Non-unit firewall:** at determinant-two, `Z^3/M^2 Z^3` can be `(Z/2)^2`, not `Z/4`; a scalar `Q_2`/one-digit carrier does not represent the same group filtration. Distinguish the computational `M`-adic carrier from actual local-field components of an adelic Rauzy representation. [Full derivation](padic-representation-literature-gate-2026-09-16.md).
4. **Degree and alphabet are different resource dimensions:** large alphabets increase prefix choices, automaton branching, legal-context checks and certificate sizes. A finite census on short ternary images is not a large-alphabet uniform bound.
5. **Countercontrols must remain:** productive recurrent cycles, projected paths that cannot be lifted to actual occurrences, finite-run caps, and full-space non-unit mismatches must all cause refusal of overstrong claims.
