## Exactly what would close the mathematics

> Every cyclic vertex of the finite **seed-reachable strict-zipper overlap graph** has a descendant with offset zero via a **composable path of actual ordered substitution occurrences**.

Quantifiers and interfaces are load-bearing:

1. Every substitution in the standing ternary PIP regime; not merely all specimens in an enumerated finite corpus or a listed parameter family.
2. Every cyclic strict-zipper **seed-reachable** vertex in that substitution's exact overlap graph. An arbitrary formal affine walk does not supply a path.
3. A valid *existential descendant* with offset zero by **ordered, occurrence-indexed, geometrically legal** children. No extra, unproved requirement that all noncoincident cycles disappear.
4. The non-unit representation must not be replaced by an unjustified unimodular or Euclidean projection. No added legality of the periodic swap seed, independent tile lengths, or injectivity assumptions.
5. The theorem must be assembled with immutable derivations, all-branch/coverage verification, exact replay/provenance and the repository's claim-governance acceptance rules. Independent human review remains explicitly pending when not performed, but is not a separate research-progress veto.
