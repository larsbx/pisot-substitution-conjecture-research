# Class-C nine-seed first inflation: targeted stop/go (2026-10-30)

Proposed scope: complete the six nonaligned canonical swap-seed first-child
interfaces in the remaining lower cone, using Theorem R_C. Then test reusable
segment transfer rules on a declared finite descendant budget. No uniform
closure or boundary-hitting conclusion is proposed from bounded exploration.

Primary sources inspected:

1. Akiyama–Lee, *Algorithm for determining pure pointedness of self-affine
   tilings* (2011), https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.2898, sections 2–4.
   Inflation of overlaps and coincidence reachability are standard. Their
   terminating algorithm requires the Meyer/realization hypotheses and a
   complete overlap collection. Nine swap seeds and one inflation alone
   do not meet that contract.
2. Akiyama–Barge–Berthé–Lee–Siegel, *On the Pisot substitution conjecture*,
   https://www.irif.fr/~berthe/Articles/AkiyamaBargeBertheLeeSiegel.pdf,
   section 5.4, Theorems 5.6–5.7. Finiteness of overlap equivalence classes
   for a fixed Meyer tiling does not provide a parameter-uniform carrier.
   Their definition uses return vectors; the repository's seed-patch graph
   still needs its separate realization bridge.
3. Akiyama, *Strong coincidence and overlap coincidence* (2016),
   https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/dcds.2016027.
   Its equivalence concerns multiple strong coincidence, sufficiently large
   powers and trivial height group in a nonperiodic self-affine setting.
   A single first-generation swap interface is not that hypothesis.

Decision: **proceed with the narrowed local target**. Segment-pair overlap
counting itself is standard; any contribution here is its exact specialization
and clipping at Class-C's sharp Perron length walls. Reuse exact field and word
primitives. The independent oracle must enumerate actual occurrence pairs,
not consume symbolic windows or a frozen graph. Reject endpoint tangencies,
retain occurrence multiplicities before deduplicating states, and distinguish
one-generation theorems from finite descendant experiments. The known growth
near the PIP boundary (side-notes ledger, 2026-10-29) rejects a constant-label
extrapolation. No claim of new general overlap machinery is made.
