# Fibonacci Hamiltonian ↔ PSC: one hull, two spectra

**Status:** research bridge only; non-load-bearing for PSC theorem status.

This note records how the Fibonacci Hamiltonian of mathematical physics sits next to the objects of this repository. It imports nothing into the PSC proof and changes no claim status in the ledger or roadmap. Every spectral statement below is a literature statement, cited and **not** gated here.

## The shared object

The Fibonacci substitution `a -> ab, b -> a` has incidence matrix `[[1, 1], [1, 0]]`, characteristic polynomial `x^2 - x - 1`, dominant root the golden mean and determinant `-1`. It is a primitive, irreducible, unimodular Pisot substitution on **two** letters, so it lies in the settled case of the [literature crosswalk](../motivations/psc-literature-crosswalk-2026-11-04.md) (Barge–Diamond, Hollander–Solomyak) and outside the three-letter corpus of `kernel/psc/corpus.mojo`. Its hull is the Sturmian subshift of slope `alpha = (sqrt(5) - 1)/2`.

## Two different spectra of that hull

| | Dynamical spectrum | Schrödinger spectrum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operator | Koopman operator of the shift on the hull | `(H u)(n) = u(n+1) + u(n-1) + lam w(n) u(n)` on `l^2(Z)`, `w` in the hull |
| What PSC calls "spectrum" | this one | not this one |
| Type | pure discrete (two-letter case of the conjecture) | purely singular continuous for every `lam != 0` [S89], [DL99] |
| Set | eigenvalue group `Z + Z alpha` mod 1 | a Cantor set of zero Lebesgue measure, depending on `lam` [S89] |
| Depends on | the hull only | the hull and the coupling |

Pure discrete spectrum of the hull is therefore compatible with, and says nothing about, the absence of eigenvalues of `H`. **No PSC result transfers to the spectral type of `H`, and none is claimed.**

## What does transfer: the labels, not the sizes

The gap-labelling theorem for substitution potentials [BBG92] places the values of the integrated density of states on the gaps of the spectrum of `H` in the module generated by the letter and word frequencies of the hull. For Fibonacci that module is `Z + Z alpha`, the same group as the dynamical eigenvalues above, and every label in it is attained by an open gap for every `lam > 0` [DGY16].

So the quantities this repository computes for a substitution (incidence matrix, Perron eigenvector, return lattice) are the ones that fix **where** gaps may sit in the integrated density of states. They do not fix the energy at which a gap sits or its length; those depend on `lam` and are governed by the trace map, which has no counterpart here.

## The finite shadow

The periodic approximant of level `k` repeats the word `W_k` (`W_0 = 0`, `W_1 = 1`, `W_(k+1) = W_(k-1) W_k`) of length `F_k` with `F_(k-1)` ones, so its letter frequency is the convergent `F_(k-1)/F_k` of `alpha`. `docs/fibonacci-hamiltonian-band-edges-spec.md` in `larsbx/finite-math-kernels` (branch `ccr-75105a60-8qpajw`, commit `9791f98`; not on `main` at the time of writing) computes certified rational enclosures of the bands and gaps of those approximants, and observes `F_k` bands at every tested level with nonzero coupling. That work is finite-domain evidence about approximants; it states no fact about the limit.

## What is not here

- No route from overlap coincidence, balanced pairs or strict zippers to any property of `H`.
- No three-letter analogue: for a three-letter Pisot substitution the transfer matrices are still in SL2, but the trace map lives on a higher-dimensional character variety and the corresponding statements are a separate literature, not surveyed here.
- No change to issue #84, #138, #139 or #461.
