# Rauzy fractals on the 2-torus: targeted literature stop/go check

**Status:** stop/go note under the review gate of `AGENTS.md`, dated 2026-10-21. It records the decision for `kernel/psc/rauzy_torus.mojo`, its driver `kernel/rauzy_torus_census.mojo`, the oracle `oracles/python/rauzy_torus.py` and the bundle `evidence/rauzy-torus-2026-10-21/`. It proves nothing, promotes no claim and changes no ledger entry. The Pisot substitution conjecture, G1 and every #138/#139 premise remain open.

## Proposed experiment

Show the repository's own corpus on the torus that the Pisot conjecture is about, using exact data rather than the floating-point JavaScript of `docs/rauzy-representability-2026-10-08.html`.

Setup:

- `σ` is a unimodular PIP substitution on `{0,1,2}`, `M` its incidence matrix, `f` its frequency vector and `p_n` the stepped-line vertices of a fixed point.
- `H = {x : Σx_i = 0}` and `H_Z = H ∩ Z³ ≅ Z²`, with basis `e₀ − e₂, e₁ − e₂`.

The emitted data, for each `σ`:

1. the certified integers `F_i = ⌊2⁴⁰ f_i⌋`;
2. the integer enclosures `2⁴⁰ D_ni ∈ [2⁴⁰ p_ni − nF_i − n, 2⁴⁰ p_ni − nF_i]` of the discrepancy vectors `D_n = p_n − n f`, each with its next letter;
3. on a `G × G` grid of `T² = H/H_Z`, the number of distinct `Z²`-lifts of each cell that the sample meets (the *lift profile*).

## Prior art and field terminology

1. Rauzy, *Nombres algébriques et substitutions*, Bull. SMF 110 (1982). Tribonacci: the fractal (Rauzy fractal) tiles the plane periodically, and the shift is measurably conjugate to a translation of `T²` by the frequency vector.
2. Arnoux and Ito, *Pisot substitutions and Rauzy fractals*, Bull. Belg. Math. Soc. 8 (2001). Under strong coincidence the domain exchange on `R = ⋃ R(i)` is a.e. a translation of `T^{d−1}`, the quotient of the contracting plane by the lattice generated by `π(e_i − e_j)`.
3. Ito and Rao, *Atomic surfaces, tilings and coincidences I*, Israel J. Math. 153 (2006); Barge and Kwapisz, *Geometric theory of unimodular Pisot substitutions*, Amer. J. Math. 128 (2006). Pure discrete spectrum ⇔ the fractal tiles periodically ⇔ the covering degree (coincidence rank) is 1 ⇔ super-coincidence or balanced-pair termination.
4. Siegel and Thuswaldner, *Topological properties of Rauzy fractals*, Mém. SMF 118 (2009). The projection along the expanding line onto the plane `Σx_i = 0`, so that `π(p_n)` is the discrepancy vector `p_n − |p_n|₁ f`. This is the standard drawing convention; tiling and covering-degree statements are invariant under the linear isomorphism with the contracting plane.
5. Akiyama, Barge, Berthé, Lee and Siegel, *Rigidity and coincidences of Pisot substitutions* (survey, 2015). Non-unimodular Pisot substitutions need a `p`-adic factor, so the quotient is a solenoid, not a torus.

## Hypotheses that transfer and hypotheses that do not

Transfer:

- *The rotation is the frequency.* For unimodular irreducible Pisot `σ`, `D_n mod H_Z = −n(f₀, f₁) mod Z²`, which is exactly the orbit of the toral rotation in items 1 and 2. The coordinates are the field's own, not a convention of this repository.
- *Exactness.* Frequencies are decided by exact sign queries in `Q(β)` (`psc.perron_field3`). An iteration hint is accepted only after the two sign queries that certify it. Cells are assigned only when the whole integer enclosure lies inside one cell, so no floating-point value enters the kernel (binding criterion C1).

Do not transfer:

- *Covering degree.* It is a measure-theoretic quantity (item 3). A finite sample at one grid resolution sees boundary cells with two or more lifts even when the covering degree is 1, and can miss lifts in cells it has not yet visited. The lift profile is therefore a diagnostic only: it neither certifies nor refutes covering degree 1, and it is not a balanced-pair or super-coincidence certificate.
- *Non-unimodular specimens.* Item 5 removes the torus picture for these, so the kernel refuses them rather than drawing a wrong quotient.
- *Shape.* The `(D₀, D₁)` picture is a linear image of the Euclidean contracting-plane picture, so lengths and angles in it carry no meaning.

## Known negative controls and counterexamples

- **Synthetic one-lift cover against a lattice-translated duplicate** (`kernel/tests/test_rauzy_torus.mojo`). The estimator returns all ones for the first and all twos for the second, so it can see a covering degree of 2 when one is present.
- **The non-unimodular substitution `0 → 011, 1 → 20, 2 → 0`** (from the 2026-10-08 page). It is refused both in the bundle and in the tests.
- **A wrong frequency hint.** Hints of −1, 0 and off-by-one values all fall back to bisection and give the same certified floor.
- **Tribonacci** (item 1, covering degree 1 is a theorem). At 8192 points on a 64-grid its profile is `1:3957 2:47 3:1`. The 48 multi-lift cells are boundary cells, which illustrates why the profile cannot certify anything.

## Decision

**Proceed, narrowed to a diagnostic and visual record.**

- The bundle has status `diagnostic`. No ledger entry, status surface or manuscript may cite a lift profile as evidence for covering degree 1.
- The bundle exists so that `larsbx/math-vizops` can draw it without deriving anything itself:
  - the Rauzy fractal in `H`-coordinates, coloured by subtile;
  - its fold onto `T²`, coloured by lift count;
  - the same fold on an embedded torus surface;
  - the corpus atlas of rotation vectors `ρ_σ = −(f₀, f₁) mod Z²`.
