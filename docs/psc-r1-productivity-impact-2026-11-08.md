## The failed implication and its replacements

Write `P(s) = ∃d, s →* d ∧ diagonal(d)`, with edges given by complete ordered irreducible factors of one inflation, and a diagonal a one-letter pair `a/a`. If `s →* t` and `P(t)`, concatenate the paths to get `P(s)`. In particular, nonproductivity is forward-closed. Strong connectivity allows a witness from one member of an SCC to serve all members. In a complete finite descendant graph, productivity of every reachable state is equivalent to each reachable **sink** SCC containing a diagonal. Every state can reach a sink, and a sink has no outgoing path.

The [literal binary control](psc-r1-productivity-scope-2026-11-07.md) gives `R = 001/100` under `0→01, 1→10`. Its full ordered row is `01/10, 0/0, 1/1, 10/01`. The two off-diagonal orientations have complete child rows confined to that two-state class. Both root words are factors of `01101001`, and the root has no interior balanced cut. Hence `P(R)` holds while both strict descendants are nonproductive. The incidence polynomial `x(x−2)` is reducible, so this refutes the unrestricted implication outside the PIP domain.

The [independent Tribonacci proof](psc-r1-common-initial-repair-2026-11-07.md) uses `0→01, 1→02, 2→0`. The two inflated tracks of **any** nonempty balanced pair begin with `0`. Positions 0 and 1 are consecutive balanced cuts, so `0/0` is a literal first child. This excludes a strictly child-closed nonproductive balanced-pair class for this substitution. It neither depends on a root witness nor classifies bare arithmetic germs as balanced-pair states.
