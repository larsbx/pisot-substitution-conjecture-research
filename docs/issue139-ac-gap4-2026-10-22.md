Three primary-source checks were repeated before this extension:

| Source | Transfer and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| [Akiyama–Lee, overlap algorithm](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1003.2898), §§3–4 | Exact affine overlap graphs and their productive exits are established tools. The tiling/Meyer spectrum theorem does not identify a swap-seed graph with all realized overlaps. |

| [Barge, initial-letter criterion](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1403.7826) | Initial-letter injectivity is required; A has `(x,c,x)` and C has `(x,c,c)`, so the theorem does not directly discharge either family. |

Decision: **proceed with two narrowed certificate targets**. For A gap four,
reuse the existing residue-ray carrier/closure/descent checker with
`n=4l+r`; fitted tables are candidates until its universal inequalities
pass. For C gap four, first try the existing complete symbolic-line
certificate separately on `(n+4,n,n-3)` and `(n+4,n,n-5)`; if it refuses,
retain the refusal and seek an occurrence-aware reduction instead of
claiming a sampled plateau. No new spectral import is proposed.

Controls: incorrect residue or determinant sign, wrong first/last letter,
deleted real child, illegal occurrence index, undecided strict sign,
target-free component and exhausted state/time budget must not pass.
Neither shared eigenvalues nor a few successive graphs prove a morphism.
No claim of full A/C coverage, universal #139, #138, #84, G1 or PSC is made.

The same domain inequalities give a useful additional C test without a new
kernel: put `d=4+a`. On the plus branch set `n=3+2a+b`, giving

This is only a certificate target: a quadrant certificate also needs its
boundary strips. A refusal or timeout is no obstruction to boundary hitting.

## Class A: closure and selected occurrence descent

**Theorem A4.** On `x -> x y^(n+4) x`, `c -> c y^n x`,
`y -> x y^(n+eps) c`, every vertex of the complete canonical swap-seed
overlap graph has an offset-zero descendant for every admissible PIP member:
`eps=+1, n>=0`, or `eps=-1, n>=3`.

For each residue `r=0,1,2,3`, write `n=4l+r`. The tables in
`kernel/psc/class_a_ray_d4p_rank.mojo` and `class_a_ray_d4m_rank.mojo` give a typed
carrier, a partition of every carrier row, a nonnegative integer affine rank
`X(l,j)`, and one ordered occurrence type for every non-target piece.
The unchanged universal checkers establish forward closure and strict rank
decrease along those occurrences. (The word “descent” below means this rank drop.)

**Proof.** Use the residue-ray reduction of
[class-a-residue-rays-2026-10-22.md](class-a-residue-rays-2026-10-22.md).
With `h=1/(beta-1)`,

```text
P(n,h)=1-(n+eps)h-(n+5-eps)h²-4h³,
L=(1+(5-eps)h+4h², 1+(1-eps)h, 1+h),
s=k+c+y,   T(w)=s L_c+4k h(1+h)+eps*y*h,
M w=(k+s, s-k, n*s+4k+eps*y).
```

The child rule is exactly `w'=Mw+prefix_bottom(j)-prefix_top(i)`.
For first/run/last segments, the checker enumerates all nine ordered pairs,
proves the run-position bounds and the strict Perron overlap inequalities,
and exhausts the child `y'` window and every gap in the proposed carrier.
The identity `4(al+b)=an+(4b-ar)` and elimination of `n h(1+h)` reduce every
sign to a polynomial in `h`. Exact Sturm signs on `(0,1/n0]` certify all
integer members of the ray, rather than only the calibration samples.
There are nine real seeds, three non-strict seed candidates and eight
zero-offset target pieces on each ray.

The potential verifier checks every piece boundary, every selected occurrence,
its exact child labels and offsets, and `X(child)<=X(parent)-1`. Therefore
induction on the nonnegative integer `X` constructs a composable path to an
offset-zero target; its length is at most `X`. The displayed bounds majorize
`X` on the whole carrier. Every admissible `1<=n<n0` is checked by a complete
screened graph; the plus member `n=0` is checked separately because the original `ALine`
coordinates required `n>=1` (main now admits that member directly). These finite prefixes and the four infinite rays
exhaust each stated line. No carrier-equals-graph claim for all n is needed:
seed containment and closure suffice. The first two members of each ray also
replay carrier equality and every chosen occurrence against the exact graph.

## Synthesis failures and negative controls

The first A candidates, fitted on `n=16..27`, failed with incomparable affine
child-piece clips and missing uniform occurrence types. Later candidates
passed all sampled steps but failed the infinite-ray verifier: negative
potential, missing partition coverage, or a potential difference with negative
eventual slope. They are synthesis failures, not failures of boundary hitting.
The packet preserves representative refusal logs and the pre-refit candidate
for `eps=+1,r=0`; its negative rank-drop slope remains an exact counter-control. In descent row
125, at `j=l-2`, `X(parent)-X(child)-1=8-l`. At `l=9, n=36`, the actual
ordered occurrence `(32,37)` sends `(y,c,8,-9,0)` to `(y,x,6,-8,1)`;
both candidate ranks are 17. The complete native export confirms that edge
and that both vertices still hit. The failed rank is not a non-hitting state.

Two synthesis refinements suffice here. `--tail-floor` separates the fitting
samples from the proposed certification tail, and rejects endpoint ranks
with negative eventual slope. For five rays, `class_a_ray_rank.py` proposes
new affine rank coefficients on the **same** pieces and occurrences. It uses
linear programming only to propose rational coefficients, scales to integers,
then requires the independent exact closure/descent verifier before writing.
The canonical Mojo checker subsequently verifies the integer tables. No
floating-point optimizer verdict enters the proof. Three rays need no rank
refit. Synthesis software and version pins are recorded for reproducibility;
only the exact integer tables and their exact checks are load-bearing.

For every A ray, native and independent controls reject a removed carrier row,
the next residue's carrier, a wrong child y-coordinate and a missing descent.
Native controls additionally reject an inflated child rank and an illegal
occurrence segment. The preceding floor on the same residue refuses; this
means the fixed certificate is not accepted there, not that the member fails
to hit. C controls check the true PIP-domain endpoints and initial/final c
letters, and reject an invalid sign, opposite-sign verdict and altered vertex
count. Existing generic symbolic-line tests guard sign and determinant errors.

The complete C residual wedge parameterizations above are valid covers, but
both direct wedge runs refuse at the first seed `0|0||{1,}|`: a run-position
bound is not affine at the samples (plus values `-606,-1007`, minus
`-612,-1013`, base 200). This is the kernel's fail-closed diagnostic; it is
not a proof that no useful partition or parametric certificate exists.

## Remaining proof obligations

The next unsolved gap on both targets is `d=5`; neither A nor C has been
exhausted. The reusable conditional statement is precise: for each admissible
gap and sign, seed-containing forward-closed carriers together with exact
occurrence choices and nonnegative integer ranks strictly decreasing off the
zero-offset targets imply eventual hitting. Residue rays and finite prefixes
may cover a line; a C quadrant proof must also cover all boundary strips.
The ranks and finite graphs may depend on parameters. A common contraction
factor, a common carrier cardinality and a uniform depth across gaps are not
premises of this implication.

This work supplies its hypotheses for four entire infinite lines only. It
does not say every possible walk reaches a target, identify unequal-letter
zero-offset targets with equal-letter coincidences, or transport graph paths
from matching characteristic polynomials. No new realization, adelic,
independence or spectrum-transfer hypothesis is discharged. Universal #139,
#138, #84, G1 and PSC remain open. Class-D accepted results are unchanged.
