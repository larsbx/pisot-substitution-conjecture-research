# Selected-rank Lean formalization: scoped stop/go

**Status:** implementation plan for the already stated finite selected-path
contract; no new universal productivity or PSC claim.

## Target and sources

Formalize the implication in [the selected-rank note](selected-rank-soundness-2026-10-31.md):
on a declared finite carrier, zero rank means a genuine terminal, and each
positive-rank vertex has a genuine child of rank exactly one less. Then it has
a terminal path of exactly its rank. Establish propagation to seed descendants
only under a separately supplied all-edge carrier-closure hypothesis.

Primary sources inspected on 2026-10-31:

1. [Lean language reference, recursive definitions](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Definitions/Recursive-Definitions/):
   structural recursion and decreasing measures supply termination arguments.
2. [Pinned Lean natural-number recursor](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/v4.34.0-rc2/src/Init/Data/Nat/Basic.lean):
   `Nat.recAux` is the standard natural-number induction interface. A unit
   decrement permits ordinary induction, with no stronger recursion principle.
3. [Pinned Mathlib relation closures](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/4edb0dbaa3b3cf729d86d1e2f035474d5ae2ac09/Mathlib/Logic/Relation.lean):
   reflexive transitive closure is generated by a reflexive case and genuine
   edges. An explicitly length-indexed finite path retains the exact rank bound.

These are standard constructions. Novelty is not claimed for rank descent or
closure induction. The implementation specializes their interface to finite
vertex tables and makes each semantic assumption visible.

## Hypotheses and refusal boundary

The proof will assume the supplied edge relation represents actual transitions
and the terminal predicate represents typed coincidences. This does not prove
that Mojo's parser, fixed-width arithmetic or occurrence checker refines Lean.
That implementation-refinement bridge remains a separate obligation.

Selected descent does not require every outgoing edge to decrease rank. Nor
does it prove that the table covers every descendant. Preserve a three-vertex
negative control: a listed source has a selected terminal child and another
child outside the two-vertex carrier, with a nonproductive self-loop there.
The local certificate accepts while all-descendant productivity fails.

## Decision

Proceed with the finite selected-path theorem, a conditional closure-transfer
theorem and the explicit negative control. Use only finite vertex types and
natural-number induction. Do not register universal X productivity, BH, G1,
PDS or PSC credit, and do not claim Mojo-to-Lean refinement from matching names.
