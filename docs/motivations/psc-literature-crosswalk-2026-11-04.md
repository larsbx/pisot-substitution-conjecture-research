**Status:** literature orientation only. This is not a new mathematical theorem, exhaustive 2026 survey, independent validation of any manuscript, or change to governed claim statuses. **The general Pisot Substitution Conjecture (PSC) remains open.** The [earlier ternary comparison](ternary-literature-context-2026-10-07.md), [proof ladder](../proof-ladder.md), [claim/source map](../claim-status-and-source-map-2026-09-13.md), and [issue #461](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/issues/461) remain authoritative for their respective scopes.

## Definition and three distinctions

In this project, primitive irreducible Pisot (PIP) means a primitive substitution whose incidence characteristic polynomial is irreducible over the rationals, with Perron eigenvalue \(\beta>1\) a Pisot number. PSC predicts pure discrete dynamical spectrum for the substitution. The symbolic shift (\(\mathbb Z\)-action), its natural-length tiling flow (\(\mathbb R\)-action), a geometric Rauzy domain exchange, and a solenoidal/toral factor are *different objects*. Passing among them requires the hypotheses of the invoked theorem; a factor or domain exchange by itself is not a demonstration of a.e. one-to-one coding or PDS.

The conjecture includes non-unimodular cases (\(|\det M|>1\)); arguments formulated only in Euclidean contracting spaces need explicit treatment of the missing finite-place information. Likewise, **relatively dense dynamical eigenvalues** are not **pure discrete spectrum**: the latter excludes a continuous spectral component. For the variants and precise connections, start with [Akiyama, Barge, Berthé, Lee and Siegel (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-0348-0903-0_2).

A Rauzy construction does not automatically resolve measurable coding multiplicity.

## Map to this repository's live obligations

1. **Reduction, not closure.** Bounded discrepancy and finite seed-patch overlap graphs are repository-proved at their stated scope. The remaining **one suitable swap-seed productivity premise [#84](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/issues/84)** is open; its ternary aligned **A1′ [#138](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/issues/138)** and strict-zipper **BH [#139](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/issues/139)** subproblems are not discharged by the two-letter theorem. The conditional density-to-tiling-PDS and flow-to-symbolic imports remain recorded in [the proof ladder](../proof-ladder.md).
2. **Finite results do not quantify over arbitrary substitutions.** [Theorem Ω](../audit-theorem-omega-2026-10-08.md) covers **145,806** substitutions in its declared bounded domain; the standing short-image corpus has **4,554**, and a separate strong-coincidence census covers **408,798** specimens. Counts and hypotheses come from [the earlier comparison](ternary-literature-context-2026-10-07.md) and [the README](../../README.md), not from the external literature.
3. **C4 is an ordered-occurrence problem.** [Issue #461](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/issues/461) assumes a **supplied finite, fully strict, child-closed** PIP component and seeks a **genuine one-step interior zero-return newborn cut** with synchronized adjacent pair. [PR #460](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/pull/460) and [PR #462](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/pull/462) give exact rank/Parikh restrictions, not synchronization. The productive PIP rank-three depth-two control blocks a naive bounded-depth inference. A *global extraction theorem* from an arbitrary failure is a **separate open premise**; do not assume it. The [ordered-word contract](../c4-ordered-word-completion-interface-2026-11-02.md) distinguishes actual child words from affine packets, genuine Barge–Diamond edges from merely compatible masks, and endpoint alignment from physical interior cuts.
4. **A new family result is still a family result.** [Merged PR #479](https://github.com/larsbx/pisot-substitution-conjecture-research/pull/479) gives parameter-uniform strong coincidence for 0→01, 1→1^a21^b, 2→012, where a,b≥0 and a+b≥1, and excludes strictly child-closed noncoincident components in that family. This does not establish PDS for all ternary PIP substitutions or universal C4.

## The 2024 general-proof claim: do not silently import

[Kentaro Nakaishi, *Pisot Substitution Conjecture and Rauzy Fractals* (2024), arXiv:2401.07771v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07771) **claims** a general proof. The project's [first-entry audit](../nakaishi-2024-first-entry-audit-2026-10-13.md) identifies an unsupported equation equating second-coordinate first hits with **joint** first hits, reconstructs the missing intersection term, and records further transfer hypotheses. This is a concrete objection to a displayed proof step, **not** a counterexample to PSC or a proof that repair is impossible. No claim of universal PDS, strong coincidence or onefold Rauzy tiling may be imported on this basis without independent validation.

### Six common overclaims to reject

- "Barge–Kwapisz solved unimodular PSC": **no**, they supply geometric equivalence criteria.
- "Pisot family/Meyer implies PDS": **no**, relatively dense eigenvalues need not exhaust the spectrum.
- "Strong coincidence/domain exchange implies PDS": **not without further hypotheses**.
- "Frettlöh–Garber proved every Pisot tiling is a regular model set": **no**, the general formulation remains conjectural.
- "Sirvent–Starosta refuted PSC": **no**, their object is a pair of substitutions and the initial-pair condition.
- "Nakaishi 2024 is an accepted settled theorem": **no**, treat its claims as disputed pending source-level resolution.
