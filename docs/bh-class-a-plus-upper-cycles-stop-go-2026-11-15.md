## Primary sources freshly inspected

1. Kirschenhofer and Thuswaldner, *Shift Radix Systems — A Survey*, RIMS
   Kokyuroku Bessatsu B46 (2014), 1–59,
   [publisher PDF](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~kenkyubu/bessatsu/open/B46/pdf/B46_001.pdf),
   Sections 1, 3 and 8. The survey distinguishes eventual periodicity from
   eventual arrival at zero and records the symmetric nearest-integer
   variant. **Transfer:** terminology and the separation between those two
   properties. **No transfer:** a general SRS classification is not a proof

   asserts no novel general SRS classification.
2. Minervino and Thuswaldner, *The geometry of non-unit Pisot substitutions*,
   Annales de l'Institut Fourier 64 (2014), 1373–1417,
   [publisher PDF](https://www.numdam.org/article/AIF_2014__64_4_1373_0.pdf),
   Sections 1, 2 and 4. Prefix-automaton edges retain an actual decomposition
   of an image word; admissible expansions are labeled walks. Nonunit
   representation spaces include non-Archimedean factors. **Transfer:**
   actual occurrence compatibility and ordered prefix bookkeeping.
   **No transfer:** inverse integrality, a unit-only argument, arbitrary
   label paths without word occurrences, or a spectral conclusion for the
   present determinant-two family.
3. Akiyama and Lee, *Algorithm for determining pure pointedness of
   self-affine tilings*,
   [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1003.2898), Sections 1 and 2.4,

   proof of pure point spectrum.

The decision is a narrowed **go**: literal local paths are useful new

## Negative controls and acceptance boundary

The retired three-RUN formula is a concrete control. At `JH=4`, its second
bottom ordinal `n+2` is LAST `c`, so its actual child has type `y/c`.
At `JH<=3` that ordinal exceeds LAST. Relabeling the child while retaining
the same occurrence is invalid.

Reject wrong types, illegal or shifted ordinals, altered full offsets,
noncomposable edges, a projected zero mistaken for a full integral zero,
truncated paths, and false original prefixes. Reverse both original
indices as well as the packet. A finite resource or arithmetic refusal
must produce no mathematical verdict. Preserve exact resource bounds.

Mojo remains canonical executable code; independently written Python adds
value only as an exact replay oracle. Lean endpoints must expose precisely
their literal path, positivity/scaling and prefix hypotheses. The general
written two-edge decrement, the eight-case formal result, native execution,
independent finite replay, fresh isolated acceptance and hosted acceptance
must retain separate scopes and receipts.

Restored source is checked against any surviving recorded digest before
reuse. Fresh execution produces new source-bound receipts; historical
reported successes whose raw files were lost are labeled as such and do
not substitute for the new receipts. Publication and integration are
handled separately from this mathematical stop/go decision.
