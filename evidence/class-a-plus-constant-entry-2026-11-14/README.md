For the literal ordered substitution

```text

n>=6, 5<=d<=n+4,
```

the [written theorem](../../docs/class-a-plus-constant-entry-2026-11-14.md)
constructs a finite actual composable path to typed coincidence from
**every strict integral overlap**. One-level literal density forces
central `y/y` entry, the signed nearest recurrence is eventually
constant, and three original RUN edges lower every admissible nonzero
constant height. Nested original proper prefixes satisfy `M^m w=P-Q`.
Neither seed reachability, cyclicity nor a finite carrier is a premise.

Five complete finite S18 certificates at `n=1..5,d=n+4`, together
with the separately authenticated inherited low-gap and four Class-A
seam-line results, complete the mathematical canonical swap-seed cover
`n>=0,1<=d<=n+4`. The all-strict-source quantifier applies only to
the new chamber. The inherited certificates retain their source and
acceptance scopes. `inherited-kernel-source-comparison.json` verifies
that all 761 inherited native source/pin files are unchanged from the
parent; identity is not a fresh execution of their certificates.

The full plus PIP domain is `n>=0,1<=d<=2n+11`. The remaining upper
range is outside the present theorem. The
[upper-target memo](../../docs/class-a-plus-upper-entry-target-2026-11-14.md)
is explicitly proposed, unexecuted, unformalized and unpromoted.
