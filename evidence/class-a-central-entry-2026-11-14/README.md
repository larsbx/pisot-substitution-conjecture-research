## Mathematical argument and its executable boundaries

The [pre-code source review](../../docs/bh-class-a-central-entry-stop-go-2026-11-14.md)
records four primary sources and the scope decision. The proof itself uses
finite word counts rather than an imported frequency or spectral theorem.

1. Every depth-two image block has a positive density gap
   `Y0-3X0-2C0=(beta-5)/beta^2`. On a growing actual common interval,
   the two partial boundary blocks contribute a fixed error. If no central
   `y/y` overlap existed, each top `x` tile could cover at most three bottom
   `y` centers and each top `c` tile at most two. The gap contradicts this
   count at sufficiently large depth. Decoding the chosen deep occurrences
   through their ancestors supplies strict composability.
2. A central `y/y` source has a genuine nearest RUN child. Its two integer
   coordinates satisfy `z_(j+2)=nearest(2h*z_j-alpha*z_(j+1))`, where
   `h=1/beta`, `0<h<1/8`, `0<alpha`, and `alpha+2h<1`. Boundedness and
   determinism give eventual periodicity; the extremal inequalities force
   every nonzero cycle to alternate between the two full states
   `+/- (y,y,H,3H,-6H)`.
3. Every such nonzero cycle has an explicit actual exit. Six small parameter
   chambers cover `KH<=3`, including the `n=6` LAST-letter exception. Four
   unbounded chambers cover `KH>=4` and splice into the previously proved
   reversed `Q_H` unit-descent suffix. Every edge retains original indices,
   child letters and full integral offsets.

The resulting nested prefixes satisfy `M^m w=P-Q` in `Z^3`. No inverse
integrality or unimodularity assumption enters. The mathematical depth
depends on the substitution and source; no parameter-independent or
source-independent depth bound is claimed.

Canonical executable entry uses a bounded actual-child search followed by
the proved central selector and cycle exits. A successful emitted transcript
is a concrete witness. State, pair, step or checked-integer limits refuse
without a mathematical negative verdict; a bounded search is not the proof
of the all-source entry quantifier. The complete written argument is over
unbounded integers.
